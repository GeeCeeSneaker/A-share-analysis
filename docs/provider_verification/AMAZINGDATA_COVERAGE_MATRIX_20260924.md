## Scope and interpretation

This matrix reconciles the Issue #86 baseline with current provider contracts, retained reports, and the local official `AmazingData==1.1.9` SDK wheel. SDK method names below were checked locally without login or network access; the vendor wheels and any raw payload remain outside GitHub. Existing account observations are reused from [`amazingdata.md`](amazingdata.md), [`capability_closure_20260911.md`](capability_closure_20260911.md), and [`remaining_capability_closure_20260912.md`](remaining_capability_closure_20260912.md). No API was re-queried merely to recreate those observations.

- `VERIFIED_AVAILABLE` means a scrubbed prior call under the Owner-confirmed intended account returned a structured response. It is historical evidence, not a guarantee that the entitlement is unchanged today. `NOT_YET_VERIFIED` means no qualifying account-call evidence was found; it does **not** mean denied. The historical trial/simulation account's denials are not attributed to the current account. No current-account family is classified `VERIFIED_DENIED/UNAVAILABLE` from the reviewed evidence.
- A successful endpoint call, including an empty result, is not semantic approval. `CapabilityStatus` remains `CANDIDATE` absent its separate governed proof. A probe is not authoritative history, and retained history is not necessarily merged/published data.
- The five evidence flags are `SDK_METHOD_EXISTS / REAL_ACCOUNT_CALL_VERIFIED / RETAINED_PROBE_EXISTS / AUTHORITATIVE_HISTORY_EXISTS / CANONICAL_OR_RESEARCH_CONSUMER_EXISTS`. `Y` = yes, `N` = no, `P` = partial/mixed or a limited/planned consumer. For `AUTHORITATIVE_HISTORY_EXISTS`, `Y` means the relevant family has the required authoritative history, `P` means only a limited facet/window is retained. A probe flag refers to a sanitized report/receipt in the repository, not public raw payload.
- Ingestion labels follow the Issue #86 vocabulary: `AUTHORITATIVE`, `PARTIAL/PROBE_ONLY`, `CONTRACT_EXISTS_NOT_BUILT`, `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.

## Priority decision

The initial hypothesis is narrowed rather than repeated:

1. **Status + limit price is the sole `CORE_NOW` missing group.** Its business facts directly affect tradability, limit breadth and execution feasibility; both Canonical domains/source policy and a mapper path already exist. The data itself is only probe-level and the unresolved key shape prevents an authoritative claim.
2. **Adjustment factors and direct corporate actions move to `NEXT` together**, not independent speculative feeds: the active R1 research contract fixes prices to `UNADJUSTED_CANONICAL` and forbids adjusted/total-return output. The group becomes core when an adjusted-return consumer is scheduled; until then the direct event-date gap remains an explicit blocker, not permission to infer events.
3. **Index and industry surfaces are `NEXT`, not `CORE_NOW`.** Their planned benchmark/Phase-1 consumers are concrete, but the R1 index panel is disabled and industry taxonomy/membership is not an active Canonical source policy. Index weights and industry daily/weights are not consumed by the current membership contract.
4. **AmazingData equity structure is `DEFER` for acquisition.** The current design chooses Tushare `daily_basic` for free-float share/turnover and uses AmazingData shares for validation; SDK availability alone does not justify a second authoritative feed.
5. Margin, ETF-specific, financial, shareholder-event, Dragon-Tiger/block and minute/Level-1 data remain `DEFER`; the design has no sufficiently specific near-term consumer or validated semantic/PIT contract that would make an access probe change the next build decision.

There is therefore no unresolved access probe in #86 whose answer would change the immediate status/limit recommendation. All unresolved endpoint families above remain `NOT_YET_VERIFIED`, not denied. **Provider calls for this matrix: 0.** No B1-B7/Production run, capability approval, data acquisition or raw-payload retention occurred.

- **Endpoint / access:** `InfoData.get_history_stock_status`, current-account access historically `VERIFIED_AVAILABLE`; an additional current-code BSE probe returned 242 rows. These calls prove endpoint reachability for those exact requests only, not full historical coverage or semantic correctness.
- **Consumer:** Canonical `fact_security_status_daily` and `fact_limit_price`; market-state tradability, ST/suspension exclusions, limit-up/down breadth and backtest execution-feasibility checks. The current R1 research panel does not yet join these facts, so do not claim a completed research feature.
- **Required history:** 2020-01-01 through 2026-06-30, matching the existing 78-month authoritative daily-bar window. Extend beyond that only if the archive window is separately advanced.
- **Pipeline fit / concrete blocker:** existing Raw → Normalized → Canonical contracts, provider facade, DTO/mapper and monthly history machinery fit; do not add a new adapter/schema framework. Before acquisition, resolve the 8/20,638 rows with missing `TRADE_DATE` and `MARKET_CODE` through a documented provider contract or keep the request blocked. Preserve request-faithful BSE old/new code mapping; do not silently drop, impute, or relabel unknown rows. The endpoint's corporate-action flags remain a separate fact domain and do not close the direct-event date semantics.
- **Storage unit:** reuse the existing month-bounded Canonical partition/manifest convention (78 `YYYY-MM` windows); no new bucket or physical-layout decision is justified.
- **Minimum acceptance proof:** exact request/universe/calendar denominator is recorded; every returned row maps to exactly one security/date and every unresolved/empty result has an explicit reason; 78 monthly status/limit Canonical partitions reconcile with eligible daily-bar/security sessions with zero unexplained missing/extra/duplicate rows; representative reviewed ST, suspension, limit-price/no-limit and BSE identity cases pass; exact replay is stable. A probe or this acceptance does not itself transition capability to `APPROVED`; any Formal B1-B7/Production authorization remains a separate scheduler gate.
